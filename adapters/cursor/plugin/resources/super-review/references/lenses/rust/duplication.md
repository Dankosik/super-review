# Rust duplication

## rust.duplication.share-knowledge

**Repeated knowledge.** Do these blocks encode one domain decision or just look
alike while serving different concepts? Identify a concrete coordinated edit
that sharing would remove and the actual callers of the shared form. Keep
independent concepts separate when combining them adds flags or trait bounds.

Search the whole snapshot beyond the assigned area for the decision's names,
literal values and call shapes, and read each match. A partition limits where a
candidate is anchored, not where its partners may be.

An exhaustive `match` or a required struct field connects its copies through a
compile error, so such a copy costs an extra edit rather than silent divergence;
judge it by that cost.

Compare implicated handwritten mechanics with existing helpers and supported
standard-library operations. Name the exact operation and why its contract fits
more directly; retain domain adaptation. A familiar loop can be clearer than a
reusable callback or macro. Do not add a crate or raise MSRV merely to save lines.

Preserve relevant ordering, ownership, failure and evaluation behavior. A
repeated match can remain clear when its cases change independently. Retaining
understandable duplication is valid; textual similarity is not a sufficient
reason to introduce an abstraction.
