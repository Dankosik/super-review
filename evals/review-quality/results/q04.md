## Recommendations

### R-001 — Use one coherent worker capture strategy

**Location:** supplied `src/worker.rs`, `launch`, specifically `let worker = Arc::clone(options)`, `let prefix = options.prefix.clone()`, and the move closure's reads of `worker.delay` and `prefix`.

The current closure retains the whole `Options` allocation and also clones its `prefix`. That makes the ownership needed by the worker harder to read. **Prefer C2:** copy the `Duration` before spawning, keep the owned prefix clone, and capture only those two values. This makes the worker's inputs explicit and removes an otherwise unnecessary strong owner. The supplied facts establish that `Options` is immutable, has no relevant additional state or custom drop, and that the worker may outlive the caller's `Arc`; the owned string and copied duration preserve that lifetime and the fixed result and delay.

**C1 is a valid alternative:** retain the cloned `Arc<Options>`, remove the separate prefix clone, and read both fields through `worker` in the closure. It avoids duplicated prefix ownership while keeping the worker tied to `Options`. Choose C1 if keeping the options as one captured configuration object is the preferred expression. Do not apply both proposals as mandatory edits: C2 removes the `worker` capture that C1 uses. Neither alternative changes the returned `JoinHandle<String>` or requires a new interface. The supplied source has no consumer or compatibility evidence that favors one capture form externally.

### R-002 — Name the publisher-handle count for what it counts

**Location:** supplied `src/broker.rs`, `State::brokers`, `Broker::new`, `Clone for Broker`, and `Drop for Broker`.

Rename the private field to `publisher_count` at all four sites, retaining its initialization, increment, decrement, and zero check. The existing name can be mistaken for `Arc` strong ownership even though a `Subscription::drop` can temporarily upgrade its `Weak` while the last `Broker` drops. The new name makes the final-publisher close condition explicit without replacing the synchronized counter with `Arc::strong_count` or changing the public `Broker` interface.

### R-003 — Give the shared close transition one private owner

**Location:** supplied `src/broker.rs`, `Broker::close`, `Drop for Broker`, and `State`.

Extract the repeated `open = false` then `subscribers.clear()` statements into a private `State::close` method, called from the same two sites while their current `Mutex` guard is held. Leave the `publisher_count == 0` condition in `Drop for Broker`. This gives the two closure paths one place to maintain the order of the state change and sender removal; the method name also makes their common intent visible. Preserve the current guard and sender-drop boundaries. The supplied Tokio fact says dropping the last sender can wake a receiver synchronously, so this extraction must not be presented as a concurrency repair or used to move `clear` across the lock boundary. It changes neither the public close result nor the drain behavior.

R-002 and R-003 can be implemented together in `src/broker.rs`; neither depends on the worker choice. R-001 is independent. There is no required ordering among these changes.
