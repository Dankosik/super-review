## C-p01-1: Decoder phases are spread across independent fields

- **Change anchor:** `src/lib.rs`, `Decoder` and `Decoder::push` in the frozen snapshot: `count: Option<u8>`, `pending: Option<(u8, u8)>`, `ended: bool`, and `failed: bool`.
- **Basis:** `rust.representation.express-concepts`.
- **Observation:** These fields jointly describe one stream phase: awaiting a count, awaiting its value, emitting accepted repetitions, ended, or failed. A reader has to infer which combinations are reachable from the branches in `push` and `finish`. That protocol is the main concept maintained by this type.
- **Remedy:** A private phase enum with associated data for the awaiting-value and emitting phases would put those states in one declaration and make transitions explicit at their uses. `max` and `reserved` can remain separate accounting fields. The strongest reason to keep the current form is that all transitions are local to two short methods, and its `Option` fields permit direct `take()` operations; an enum may add matching and assignment code. The enum still offers a supported clarity gain because it removes the need to reconstruct the phase from several fields.
- **Preserve:** The public types and signatures; zero-budget and terminal-state precedence; retained count and repetitions; cumulative reservation; first-error behavior; and end-marker handling. The proposed internal representation has not been compiled or tested.
- **Affected files:** Inspected `src/lib.rs`; `Cargo.toml` supplies Rust 2024 and MSRV 1.99 context. No additional consumer context is needed for a private representation proposal.
