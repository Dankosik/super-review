### C-p02-1: Decoder progress is spread across mutually dependent fields

- **Change anchor:** Frozen `src/lib.rs`, `Decoder` fields `count: Option<u8>`, `pending: Option<(u8, u8)>`, `ended: bool`, and `failed: bool`, with their transitions in `Decoder::push` and `Decoder::finish`.
- **Basis:** `rust.representation.express-concepts`.
- **Observation:** These fields collectively describe one decoder phase: reading a count, waiting for its value, emitting a reserved run, ended, or failed. A reader must trace the methods to learn that a retained count and pending output do not coexist, and that ended and failed take precedence over active processing. The representation itself permits combinations the implementation does not use.
- **Remedy:** A private enum carrying phase data could put those states in one place, for example `ReadingCount`, `ReadingValue(u8)`, `Emitting { remaining, value }`, `Ended`, and `Failed`. Matching that phase in `push` would make the transition and its data explicit. The strongest case for keeping the fields is that the present branches follow wire order and are short; the enum would add matching and transition code. It would, however, remove the need to reconstruct the mutual exclusions across four fields.
- **Preserve:** Keep the public signatures, zero-budget and terminal-state precedence, deferred output, first-error behavior, and exact consumed-byte accounting. The proposal is structural; equivalence is unverified.
- **Affected files:** Inspected `src/lib.rs`; no other supplied consumer context is needed for the private representation.

### C-p02-2: The byte cap is represented by two values used only as one remaining budget

- **Change anchor:** Frozen `src/lib.rs`, `Decoder` fields `max` and `reserved`, and the sole cap operation in `Decoder::push`: `if count > self.max - self.reserved` followed by `self.reserved += count`.
- **Basis:** `rust.representation.express-concepts`.
- **Observation:** Every use needs the difference between the two fields. Maintaining both leaves their relationship implicit across calls, while neither original value has another supplied use.
- **Remedy:** Store a private `remaining_budget: usize`, initialized from the constructor argument; reject when `count > remaining_budget`, then subtract the accepted count. This directly names the available capacity and removes the coordinated update. The strongest reason to keep `max` and `reserved` would be a need to report the original cap or total reserved bytes, but no such use appears in the supplied source.
- **Preserve:** Reserve the full pair before emitting any of it, maintain the inclusive cap across calls, and preserve behavior at `usize::MAX`. Arithmetic and behavioral equivalence are unverified.
- **Affected files:** Inspected `src/lib.rs`; no other supplied consumer context is needed for these private fields.
