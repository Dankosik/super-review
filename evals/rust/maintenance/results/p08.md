## C-p08-1: The shared closure transition has two owners

- **Change anchor:** Frozen `src/lib.rs`, `impl Drop for Broker` and `Broker::close`: both set `state.open = false` and call `state.subscribers.clear()`.
- **Basis:** `rust.data.make-transformations-visible`; lifecycle-ownership profile.
- **Observation:** Final publisher drop and explicit close perform the same lifecycle transition in separate methods. A reader must compare both sites to establish that they reject new work and release senders so existing receivers can drain and terminate. A future change to that transition would require coordinated edits.
- **Remedy:** Put those two statements in a private `State::close` method and call it at both existing sites while holding the state guard. This gives the transition one owner. The strongest keep alternative is that the current two statements are short and visible at each call site; that does not remove the need to keep their lifecycle behavior aligned.
- **Preserve:** Keep the publisher count check in `Broker::drop`, idempotent explicit close, and sender removal at the existing guarded boundary. Do not move `clear` or publication delivery outside the lock as part of this readability change: sender destruction and `send` may synchronously wake caller code, and a different boundary would require separate ordering and reentry analysis. Behavioral equivalence is untested.
- **Affected files:** Supplied `src/lib.rs` only. Remaining consumer context: none needed for this private-method proposal.

## C-p08-2: The publisher count’s name obscures what it counts

- **Change anchor:** Frozen `src/lib.rs`, `State::brokers`, incremented in `Broker::clone`, decremented in `Broker::drop`, and initialized to `1` in `Broker::new`.
- **Basis:** `rust.data.make-transformations-visible`; lifecycle-ownership profile.
- **Observation:** `brokers` counts live publisher handles, rather than ownership of the shared allocation. That distinction matters because `Subscription::drop` can temporarily upgrade its `Weak` reference, while subscriptions must not keep publication authority alive. The current name makes readers infer this distinction from the clone and drop implementations.
- **Remedy:** Rename the private field to `publisher_count` or `broker_handles`. The count and its transitions remain as they are. The strongest keep alternative is that `brokers` corresponds to the public type name and its increments and decrements are nearby, but it leaves the resource-versus-publisher distinction implicit.
- **Preserve:** Retain the separate count; substituting `Arc::strong_count` would count temporary `Weak` upgrades and would not express the final-publisher boundary. No public API changes. Behavioral equivalence is untested.
- **Affected files:** Supplied `src/lib.rs` only. Remaining consumer context: none needed for this private-field rename.
